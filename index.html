Project 01 | Transport Analytics & Applied AI

Victorian Transport Intelligence & Agentic AI Reporting Assistant

Built an AI-powered analytics and reporting assistant using Victorian VISTA 2024–25 survey data, enabling users to ask natural-language questions and generate aggregate transport reports.

52,657Combined Dataset Records
19,864Trip Observations
5Source Datasets

Developed a multi-agent workflow with a SQL Analyst for read-only PostgreSQL queries and an ETL Analyst for data quality checks and aggregate CSV reporting. Implemented SQL structural validation, restricted database access and approved reporting tools.

Key Business Insights

Analysed transport modes, regional trip counts and travel times. Vehicle Driver was the most frequently recorded mode, with 10,454 trip observations.

Recorded an unweighted mean travel time of 18.30 minutes and a median of 11 minutes across 19,864 valid trip observations.

These are unweighted survey sample results, not population-wide estimates. Combined records are not unique survey participants.

Project 02 | Local Government Analytics

Victorian Council Financial Intelligence

Developed an end-to-end financial reporting solution analysing 79 Victorian councils across five financial years, from 2020-21 to 2024-25.

79 Victorian Councils
5 Financial Years
3 Dashboard Pages

Built Databricks and PySpark pipelines for data ingestion, cleaning and validation. Developed SQL-based financial KPIs, dimensional models, council benchmarking and executive reporting dashboards.

Key Business Insights

Analysed financial performance across 79 Victorian councils over five years.

Identified 9 councils for high analytical priority and 13 requiring financial review using project-defined screening criteria.

Benchmarked financial KPIs against council-category peer medians.

Reported 99.75% completeness across 1,580 expected KPI observations.
